## 2. Competitors and adjacent products in "edit videos with AI agents / MCP" (2026)

### Takeaway
The market splits into five layers: (a) **free open-source MCP bridges** to desktop NLEs (Resolve, Premiere, AE, CapCut drafts, ffmpeg); (b) **official hosted MCPs from SaaS editors** (Descript, Runway, OpusClip, Tella, invideo, Selects) billed through their existing subscriptions and credits; (c) **MCP-native startups** that sell render minutes (Cut Express, ChatCut); (d) **free and paid Claude Code skill packs** for talking-head reels, sold as one-time digital products from $1 to $99 (Ajene Pinnock $99, ReleaseReel $29, Editing OS ₹1,499); and (e) **code-based motion-graphics engines** (Remotion's official skills, HeyGen HyperFrames). Clipping incumbents (OpusClip, Submagic, Captions, CapCut) still own the mass market on monthly SaaS.

## 3. What's commoditized vs. what people actually pay for

### Takeaway
The plumbing is commodity: the MCP bridges to Resolve, Premiere, ffmpeg and CapCut drafts, the Remotion and HyperFrames render engines, and basic "talking head to captioned reel" skills all exist free on GitHub, often MIT or Apache. People pay for **(1) a finished, opinionated style** (named edit styles, "your own design system"), **(2) packaging and onboarding for non-developers** (handbooks, copy-paste prompts, email setup help), **(3) hosted compute and AI** (render minutes, transcription, voices), and **(4) integration into an existing paid editor's workflow and credits** (Descript, Runway).

### Cited Findings
- Free and open source: the Resolve, Premiere and FFmpeg MCPs are "free and open source, but need the editor installed" — [Tella blog](https://www.tella.com/blog/best-mcp-servers-for-video-editing); the Remotion skills are free for individuals — [Remotion docs](https://www.remotion.dev/docs/ai/coding-agents); [orshot](https://orshot.com/blog/remotion-alternatives); there are multiple free talking-head reel skills — [open-edits](https://github.com/kevinbadi/open-edits); [tenfoldmarc](https://github.com/tenfoldmarc/video-edit-skill); [griffinwooldridge](https://github.com/griffinwooldridge/talking-head-editor); one roundup lists "8 Free Skills That Do the Work" — [dariusreed](https://www.dariusreed.com/claude-video-editing-skills/)
- Paid for **styles**: Pinnock charges $99 for "20 Proven Edit Styles" — [Pinnock](https://editing.ajenepinnock.com/); Editing OS sells "your own fonts, colours, and reference edits" and a design system — [Yonko](https://yonkomedia.com/editing-os)
- Paid for **packaging and onboarding**: Editing OS's handbook, 11 copy-paste prompts and email setup help — [Yonko](https://yonkomedia.com/editing-os); ReleaseReel's five skills that "share the same studio and hand work to each other" — [ReleaseReel](https://release-reel.lndev.me/)
- Paid for **hosted render and AI**: Cut Express meters render minutes, with credits for voices, SFX and transcripts — [Cut Express](https://cut.express/); Selects credits for footage analysis — [Selects](https://tryselects.com/mcp/); Descript media minutes and AI credits — [Descript Help](https://help.descript.com/api-and-mcp/mcp-custom)
- Paid for **reviews and QA, assets and updates**: HookMaster Motion bundles film reviews, music tracks and "lifetime access and every update" — [HookMaster Motion](https://tryhookmaster.com/motion)
- Hidden cost is always the buyer's own Claude plan (and sometimes transcription), which skill sellers explicitly exclude — [Pinnock](https://editing.ajenepinnock.com/); [Yonko](https://yonkomedia.com/editing-os)

### Inferences
- A pure "MCP for X editor" product is hard to charge for unless it's hosted or significantly more reliable than the free bridges. Paid value sits in taste (styles), reliability (QA loops), less setup friction, and hosted compute that avoids Claude usage limits and local installs.
- One-time packs carry no update obligation, but buyers churn when the agent stack changes. A subscription is justified by an ongoing style library, updates as Claude and MCP change, and a community.

### Gaps
- No public sales or revenue data beyond Yonko's self-reported 500–510 buyers.
- No data on conversion from free open-source skills to paid offers.

### Takeaway
I didn't get Reddit threads (r/editors, r/VideoEditing, r/ClaudeAI): several targeted searches returned only blogs. The evidence that did come back points to consistent pains: **setup friction** (Node, ffmpeg, CEP bridges, debug-mode extensions, Claude Code on desktop only), **Claude usage limits and token cost**, **fragile or incomplete NLE automation**, **output that looks generic or "not beautiful"**, and **no native perception of footage** (needs external transcripts; frame sampling is expensive).

### Cited Findings
- **Usage limits and token cost:** Editing OS itself warns that heavy sessions hit limits — [Yonko](https://yonkomedia.com/editing-os). One Max-plan user reports about 10% of a $200/mo Max plan's 5-hour session limit for one 37-second clip across four render passes, and advises reviewing a storyboard before rendering — [clixie.ai](https://www.clixie.ai/blog/claude-code-video-editing). Sampling one frame every 2 seconds of a 10-minute video is 300 frames, or about 470k–800k tokens — [sprites.ai](https://www.sprites.ai/claude-video-editing). One Substack reports a video taking about 15,000 Opus tokens and "15 minutes of Claude Code cooking" — [Substack note](https://substack.com/@techysurgeon/note/c-231237268)
- **Quality:** a Remotion and Claude Code result was described as "not beautiful, but pretty damn good"; the author still prefers other tools for higher-quality, controllable output — [Substack note](https://substack.com/@sabrinaramonov/note/c-230788765). The Editing OS founder concedes "things still break" — [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/akshayshirsagar/)
- **No native perception:** Claude Code is "an orchestration layer," can't transcribe audio or cut frame-precisely on its own, and needs an external transcript — [sprites.ai](https://www.sprites.ai/claude-video-editing)
- **NLE bridge fragility:** in the Premiere MCP, text and graphics overlays don't work, APIs are buggy or version-dependent, CEP is deprecated, and installation requires unsigned extensions in debug mode — [Glama README](https://glama.ai/mcp/servers/@hetpatel-11/Adobe_Premiere_Pro_MCP/blob/b9fa9862ecb7be8d717cd63c4b32be594e13bd6c/README.md). Claude in Premiere is "a build-it-yourself project, not a download" (vendor guide) — [premierecopilot](https://www.premierecopilot.com/en/blog/how-to-use-claude-ai-in-premiere-pro)
- **Setup friction:** Remotion needs Node 18+ and FFmpeg, and its license may require a company license — [Glama clean-cut-mcp](https://glama.ai/mcp/servers/endlessblink/clean-cut-mcp). Editing OS needs desktop Claude Code and local tools, with no mobile option — [Yonko](https://yonkomedia.com/editing-os). Descript requires enabling network egress and code execution in Claude settings for custom setups, and YouTube links aren't supported yet — [Descript Help](https://help.descript.com/api-and-mcp/mcp-custom). CapCut has no official API, so community tools only write drafts — [usecarly](https://www.usecarly.com/blog/capcut-mcp/)
- **Pricing confusion:** sources disagree on Descript MCP cost, ChatCut tiers and Submagic add-ons — [creativeclaw](https://creativeclaw.co/claude/best-video-plugins/); [overlap.ai](https://overlap.ai/blogs/opusclip-alternatives)
- **Refund risk for buyers:** Editing OS offers no change-of-mind or "difficulty using it" refunds — [Yonko](https://yonkomedia.com/editing-os)
- **Older context (Feb 2025, single user):** an Adobe forum report of slow Premiere AI processing — [Adobe Community](https://community.adobe.com/t5/premiere-pro-discussions/ai-powered-tools/td-p/15126524)

### Inferences
- The strongest wedges for vibeediting.app: (1) a guided installer or hosted mode that avoids Claude usage-limit pain (offload transcription and render outside Claude's context); (2) storyboard approval before render, which cuts re-render waste; (3) a curated, named style library plus a "clone my reference reel" flow; (4) export to Premiere, Resolve or FCP via XML/EDL rather than fragile live bridges; (5) a fair refund or trial policy to stand apart from one-time sellers.

### Gaps
- No direct Reddit or X complaint threads were retrieved. Several searches returned only blogs and vendor content, and Reddit wasn't fetchable. A manual Reddit/X sweep is recommended before treating these themes as validated customer voice.
