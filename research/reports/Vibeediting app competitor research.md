# Sell editing styles, not MCP plumbing

Both competitors sell a **one-time product that plugs into the buyer's own AI agent**. Neither earns monthly revenue from it yet. Yonko Media's "Creator Editing OS" costs **₹1,499 (about $17), paid once**. It began as a playbook (a handbook, 11 prompts and a personal "design system") and is now an MCP that turns Claude into a talking-head reel editor. HookMaster sells a **$9–$69/month video-review SaaS**. Next to it, it sells **HookMaster Motion**, a one-time "lifetime unlock" MCP for Claude Code and Codex that makes launch films in Remotion or HyperFrames. The basic parts are now free: open-source MCPs for Resolve, Premiere and ffmpeg, Remotion's official agent skills, and many free "talking head to reel" skills on GitHub. People pay for **a finished style, easy setup, hosted compute, and updates**. So vibeediting.app should be built as **one MCP engine (a local part for your files plus an online account service), shipped as a Claude plugin with skills**. The paid features should live on your server, gated by a subscription. Do not sell plain skill files alone, because anyone can copy them. The best way to earn monthly recurring revenue (MRR) is a **hybrid model**: a free starter, a low-price one-time pack for India and impulse buyers, and a **Pro subscription** that pays for hosted transcription, new styles every month, updates and community. Before you build, sort out four things. First, Anthropic's directory **bans AI video, audio and image generation tools**. Second, Stripe India is invite-only, so you will likely need a Merchant of Record. Third, Claude usage limits are a real problem for users. Fourth, your buyers must already pay at least **$20/month for Claude Pro**.

Every fact about HookMaster and Yonko comes from search-engine extracts of their live pages and from the founders' LinkedIn profiles. **Nobody could find any content for tryhookmaster.com/editing**, and HookMaster Motion's dollar price was not visible. ## Two Indian solo-style competitors, both selling one-time access

### HookMaster: a review SaaS that added an MCP for coding agents

HookMaster's main site is a web app for **AI video QA and feedback** on short videos (TikTok, Reels, Shorts). You upload an MP4 or MOV of up to 90 seconds. The AI checks it "frame-by-frame" and gives timestamped notes on hook strength, pacing, retention and visuals ([tryhookmaster.com](https://tryhookmaster.com/)). On top of this sit "Fix Studio", which replaces weak clips with generated video from models like Kling, Veo and Seedance, and "Style Match", which turns a reference video into "a detailed blueprint" of its pacing, cuts, color and text placement ([tryhookmaster.com](https://tryhookmaster.com/)). It also sells to agencies. It checks every edit "against your client's brand guidelines, frame by frame" ([tryhookmaster.com](https://tryhookmaster.com/)). The SaaS costs **$9 (Starter), $19 (Growth) and $69 (Studio) per month**, plus one-time credit packs from about $5 to $115. Monthly credits reset each cycle, and top-up packs stay valid for a year ([pricing](https://tryhookmaster.com/pricing)). Free users only see sample results and guided tours.

**HookMaster Motion** (/motion) is the part closest to you. It calls itself "an MCP that gives Claude Code and Codex the motion design skills they do not have on their own". It walks the agent through "the story, the storyboard, the music, the cut and a full review" ([motion](https://tryhookmaster.com/motion)). The user's agent rebuilds the product screens as code in **Remotion or HyperFrames**. It renders on the user's own computer, and "Your film never leaves your machine". The user approves the story, the storyboard and the cut before anything renders ([motion](https://tryhookmaster.com/motion)). HookMaster Music scores the film *before* the visuals are built, so the cut is timed to the track ([updates](https://tryhookmaster.com/updates)). Setup takes one command: add the MCP, open Claude Code, type `/mcp`, choose `hookmaster`, and sign in ([motion](https://tryhookmaster.com/motion)). Motion is a **one-time "lifetime unlock"**. It includes 10 film reviews (7 Lite, 3 Pro), 10 music tracks and "lifetime access and every update", and it is priced against the claim that "One agency demo video costs $1,000 to $5,000" ([motion](https://tryhookmaster.com/motion)). The price itself was not found. The FAQ asks what happens when the reviews and music run out. This suggests paid top-ups later, so the "one-time" price probably also has a usage-based part.

The /editing page could not be found anywhere. The updates page says Motion "also edits your own footage and music-led edits" ([updates](https://tryhookmaster.com/updates)), so /editing is probably the same MCP aimed at editing real footage. That is a guess. HookMaster looks like a **solo project by Pranav Joshi in Lucknow**, a "video editor and solo builder" ([LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/pranav-joshi-2a198a28a/)). The SaaS was live by March 2026, and Motion launched in October 2026. The researchers found no testimonials, user numbers, Reddit or Product Hunt coverage, or tutorial library.

### Yonko Media Editing OS: a playbook turned into an MCP, sold for ₹1,499

Yonko Media is mainly a **performance-marketing agency** that runs paid ads and creative strategy for e-commerce brands ([yonkomedia.com](https://yonkomedia.com/)). Its "Creator Editing OS" page is titled "An editing system for creators". It promises that it "turns Claude into your video editor": you type what you want in plain English and get back reels with captions and motion graphics in your own style ([Editing OS](https://yonkomedia.com/editing-os)). The core idea is the buyer's **own design system** of fonts, colours, timing and reference reels. Every edit checks this system first ([Editing OS](https://yonkomedia.com/editing-os)). The product lists "a step-by-step handbook, 11 copy-paste prompts, your own design system, Windows & Mac support". It needs a computer that "can run Claude Code and the local editing tools", plus a Claude subscription bought separately ("Pro works just fine"). The page warns that "heavy editing sessions can hit usage limits" ([Editing OS](https://yonkomedia.com/editing-os)). The selling point is **no per-video credits and no extra AI tools**.

The price is **₹1,499, one time**, with an urgency line, "One more week at ₹1,499". Support is by email. The refund policy is strict: no refunds for a change of mind "or difficulty using it" ([Editing OS](https://yonkomedia.com/editing-os)). The page claims "510 creators already editing". The founder, Akshay Shirsagar, wrote on LinkedIn that "500 creators bought our talking-head editing tool in 3 weeks". He said the team turned the playbook into an MCP "so anyone could plug it into Claude or ChatGPT", and admitted "things still break, and we're fixing stuff every day" ([LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/akshayshirsagar/)). If true, that is about **₹7.5 lakh (about $9,000) in three weeks**. These numbers are self-reported and not checked. The page names no editing app (no Premiere, Resolve or Remotion), and the checkout platform is unknown.

### The wider market has five layers, and the bottom one is free

HookMaster and Yonko sit inside a crowded field. **Free open-source MCP bridges** already control DaVinci Resolve (one repo is reported at 3,422 stars), Premiere Pro and ffmpeg ([a1adams comparison](https://github.com/a1adams/mcp-servers-for-video-editing); [Tella blog](https://www.tella.com/blog/best-mcp-servers-for-video-editing)). **Big SaaS editors** now ship official MCPs inside their normal subscriptions. Descript has a hosted MCP connector in Anthropic's directory that bills media minutes and AI credits ([Descript MCP](https://www.descript.com/mcp)). Adobe launched an "Adobe for Creativity" connector in Claude on 28 April 2026 with 50+ tools, though its Premiere depth is reportedly limited ([Adobe blog](https://blog.adobe.com/en/publish/2026/04/28/adobe-for-creativity-connector)). **MCP-first startups** sell render time: Cut Express is $19/month (Pro) and $49/month (Ultra), metered by render minutes ([Cut Express](https://cut.express/)). ChatCut sells a Claude Code plugin with paid tiers, but sources disagree on its price ([ChatCut](https://chatcut.io/claude-code-plugin)). **Skill packs** sell one-time: Ajene Pinnock's "20 Proven Edit Styles" for $99 ([editing.ajenepinnock.com](https://editing.ajenepinnock.com/)), ReleaseReel's five skills for $29 ([ReleaseReel](https://release-reel.lndev.me/)), and Editing OS for ₹1,499. Many free GitHub skills do the same job, such as open-edits, video-edit-skill and talking-head-editor ([open-edits](https://github.com/kevinbadi/open-edits); [talking-head-editor](https://github.com/griffinwooldridge/talking-head-editor)). Finally, **code-based render engines** are free for individuals: Remotion's official agent skills and HeyGen's HyperFrames ([Remotion docs](https://www.remotion.dev/docs/ai/coding-agents)).

This tells you where the money is. The basic plumbing is free. Buyers pay for four things: **a finished style** (named edit styles, "your own design system"), **easy setup for non-developers** (handbooks, prompts, setup help), **hosted compute** (render minutes, transcription), and **a place inside a workflow they already pay for** (Descript, Runway). No company in this research clearly combines a strong style system with reliable hosted features and export to Premiere or Resolve for talking-head creators. That gap is where vibeediting.app fits.

## Call it a plugin, build it as an MCP

The words mean different things, and you will use all of them.

| Word | What it really is | Where it works |
|---|---|---|
| **MCP server** | The engine: a program that gives an AI agent tools (cut, caption, render) | Any MCP client: Claude Code, Claude Desktop, Cursor, Codex, ChatGPT |
| **Connector** | Anthropic's name for a *remote* (online) MCP server added in Claude | Claude web, desktop and mobile, plus Claude Code |
| **Plugin** | A bundle of skills, commands, agents, hooks and MCP settings | Claude Code, Claude chat and Cowork (parts vary by surface) |
| **Skill** | A folder with a SKILL.md file of instructions, plus scripts | About 30 agent tools (open standard) |
| **Desktop extension (.mcpb)** | A one-click package of a local MCP for Claude Desktop | Claude Desktop only, and no longer accepted in Anthropic's directory |

Anthropic defines a connector as "an MCP server", and remote connectors must use Streamable HTTP and OAuth 2.0 for sign-in ([Claude docs: Submit a connector](https://claude.com/docs/connectors/building/submission); [Software Directory Policy](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13145358-anthropic-software-directory-policy)). A plugin is the main unit Anthropic now distributes. Skills load in chat, Cowork and Claude Code. But a **local MCP server is "Ignored" in Claude web chat**, and works in Cowork only when the session runs on your own computer ([Platform support](https://claude.com/docs/plugins/platform-support.md)). Anthropic "no longer accepts MCPB submissions" to its directory and says to put local servers inside a plugin instead ([MCPB docs](https://claude.com/docs/connectors/building/mcpb.md)). Skills are not a separate submission type either. You "bundle them in a plugin" ([Submit a connector](https://claude.com/docs/connectors/building/submission)).

The most important technical fact for a video product is this: **a remote MCP server cannot read the user's hard disk**. Raw footage, ffmpeg and editing apps like Resolve or Premiere all live on the user's computer. Every video MCP the researchers found runs locally and takes file paths as inputs ([MCPB docs](https://claude.com/docs/connectors/building/mcpb.md); [ffmpeg-mcp listing](https://enterprisedna.co/directories/mcp/video-creator-ffmpeg-mcp)). So the product must have a **local part**. A purely online connector would only work if users upload footage, or if you only work on transcripts and send back an edit list.

**Recommendation:** build **one MCP engine with two halves**. The **local executor** is a local MCP server that runs ffmpeg, transcription, captions, rendering and export to Premiere or Resolve. The **online service** is a remote MCP or API with OAuth. It handles accounts, subscription checks, the style library, hosted transcription and any other paid intelligence. Ship it in three wrappers. (1) A **Claude plugin** that bundles your skills and points to both halves. This is your main product. (2) The same **skills folder** published for Codex, Cursor and Gemini CLI. (3) The local executor on **npm or PyPI**, so any MCP client can install it. Anthropic's own advice for a product is to make two directory submissions: the server as an MCP connector, and the plugin "whose skills teach Claude how to use it" ([Publish to the directory](https://claude.com/docs/directory/publish.md)).

For naming, on your website, call it **"the vibeediting plugin for Claude Code (and other AI agents)"**. Mention "MCP" in the second line, because early buyers search for that word, and both competitors use it. Creators who are not technical understand "plugin" better than "MCP". Never put "Claude" in the product name, logo or domain. Write "works with Claude" instead. Anthropic's trademark guidelines govern its marks, and the name `claude-plugins-official` is reserved ([trademark guidelines](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/trademark-guidelines); [Create a marketplace](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/plugin-marketplaces)).

## Gate the server, not the files

Anything you deliver as a text file can be copied. Skills and prompts are plain Markdown. A plugin listed in Anthropic's directory **must come from a public GitHub repo** ([Submit your plugin](https://claude.com/docs/plugins/submit.md)). The directory policy also forbids hidden or encoded instructions, and forbids pulling behaviour instructions from your server at run time ([Software Directory Policy](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13145358-anthropic-software-directory-policy)). So you cannot hide paid prompts inside a listed plugin. The only strong lock is **on your server**: the user signs in, and your server checks their subscription on every paid call.

| Gating method | How it works | Good for | Copy / piracy risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Remote MCP + OAuth + subscription check** | Server checks the paid plan on each call | Paid features (styles, transcription, cloud render) | Low (only account sharing) |
| **Local MCP + license key** | Key checked online, with offline grace period | Unlocking the local executor | Medium–high (code can be patched) |
| **Private GitHub repo access** | Buyer is invited to a private plugin repo | Developers only | High; buyers need GitHub plus git login |
| **Private "archive" marketplace with a token** | Plugin zip downloaded with a bearer token from a helper command | Non-developers, revocable updates | High once downloaded |
| **Pay-per-call (x402, USDC)** | Agent pays per request in crypto | Developer tools | Poor fit for creators |
| **Skill pack sold as a download** | Gumroad / Lemon Squeezy file | Lead magnet | Trivial to share |

Claude Code supports self-hosted plugin marketplaces. A private repo works, but "Claude Code has no git token of its own", so each buyer needs their own GitHub login ([Host a marketplace](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/plugins/host-marketplace.md)). A better option for creators is an **`archive` source**: a zip file over HTTPS, protected by a `headersHelper` command that prints a bearer token from the user's license file. This needs Claude Code v2.1.238 or newer, and the user must accept the helper command ([Host a marketplace](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/plugins/host-marketplace.md)). For Anthropic connectors, the portal can hold your OAuth client. It can also ask for sign-in only "when the server asks", so some tools can be free and others paid ([Submit a connector](https://claude.com/docs/connectors/building/submission)). The listing form has a field for "what users need before they can connect, such as accounts, plans", so a connector can clearly require a paid account.

**Recommendation:** keep the local executor and the skills **free or open-source**, and make them useful on their own. They are your marketing. Put the paid value **on your server**: the curated style library and new monthly styles, "clone my reference reel" analysis, hosted transcription, cloud render, team brand kits and usage metering. Gate all of it with OAuth plus a subscription check. Use a license key only as an easy way to link the local tool to the user's account. Use the private archive marketplace for any paid-only plugin pieces you do not want in the public repo.

## A hybrid model is the realistic route to MRR

Comparable creator tools all charge monthly or yearly. **OpusClip** is $15–$29 a month and meters by processing minutes. **Submagic** is $19–$69 a month ($12–$41 if billed yearly) and meters by finished videos. **Descript** is $24–$65 a month ([forasoft](https://www.forasoft.com/learn/ai-for-video-engineering/articles-ai/opus-clip-descript-submagic-captions-ai-video-editor-tools-2026); [techpilot](https://techpilot.ai/tools/submagic/)). Asset libraries like Envato and Motion Array cost about $16–$40 a month ([photutorial](https://photutorial.com/best-envato-alternatives/)). Claude skill packs, in contrast, mostly sell one-time for **$5–$30**, and most skills are free ([kissmyskills](https://kissmyskills.com/blogs/news/best-claude-skills-marketplaces-2026)). Buyers accept a subscription when something **keeps costing money or keeps changing**. Subscriptions win in "fast-moving categories. AI models... and anything tied to third-party APIs need continuous updates" ([riskverdict](https://www.riskverdict.com/guides/lifetime-deal-vs-subscription)). Your product fits this exactly. Claude, MCP, Premiere and Resolve all change often, and Yonko's founder already admits his one-time product "still breaks".

The money difference is large. One example: a $29/month plan kept for 18 months is worth about **$522 per customer**, while a $199 lifetime deal is worth $199 ([Zanfia](https://zanfia.com/blog/lifetime-deal-vs-monthly-subscription-which-sells-more-digital-products-2026/)). One-time deals do bring fast early cash. Typical B2C subscription churn is **5–7% per month** ([koji.so](https://www.koji.so/blog/saas-churn-rate-benchmarks-2026)). At 5% monthly churn, about 54% of a group of buyers is still paying after 12 months ([mean.ceo](https://blog.mean.ceo/subscription-churn-retention-benchmarks-statistics/)). One weak source says yearly-plan customers churn about half as much as monthly ones ([pmtoolkit](https://pmtoolkit.ai/benchmarks/churn-rate-benchmarks)), so push yearly plans. Be careful with "lifetime" promises. Filmora moved lifetime buyers to a subscription in 2022, faced a backlash, and had to reopen the old deal ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wondershare_Filmora)).

Hosting costs are low if you avoid cloud rendering. Transcription costs about **$0.15–$0.36 per audio hour** with AssemblyAI, Deepgram or OpenAI Whisper ([futureagi](https://futureagi.com/blog/speech-to-text-apis-in-2026-benchmarks-pricing-developer-s-decision-guide/); [l8bites](https://l8bites.com/blog/ai-transcription-tools-compared-whisper-deepgram-assemblyai-gladia-2026)). A heavy user transcribing 20 hours a month costs you about $3–7. On a $15 plan, after payment fees, you still keep more than 65%. Cloud rendering and AI video generation are the real cost risks, and the research did not price them, so sell them only as credits. Remember that the buyer also pays Anthropic. **Claude Code is not in Claude's free plan.** Pro is $20/month, and Max is $100 or $200 ([noqta](https://www.noqta.tn/blog/claude-code-pricing-2026)).

**Suggested price ladder to test** (these are starting points, not proven numbers):

| Tier | Global price | India price (suggested) | What it includes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Free** | $0 | ₹0 | Local executor, 1–2 basic styles, tutorials |
| **Style Pack** (one-time) | $29–$49 | ₹1,499–₹1,999 | Fixed set of styles + 12 months of updates; matches Yonko's price point in India |
| **Pro** (subscription) | $15/mo or $129/yr | about ₹599/mo or ₹4,999/yr | Full style library, a new style every month, hosted transcription (fair-use limit), "clone my reel", community, updates |
| **Studio / Agency** | $49–$69/mo | – | Seats, shared brand kits for clients, priority support |
| **Credits** | Packs | Packs | Cloud render, heavy transcription, any extra compute |

This way, impulse buyers in India can still buy once, as with Yonko, while the Pro plan gives you MRR. Make the Pro benefits *visibly recurring*, such as a new style every month, so the subscription does not feel like paying monthly for a static file. If you want a launch cash event, a **limited** early-adopter lifetime deal is fine. Cap the quantity, exclude hosted compute, and write clear terms.

## What to settle before writing code

### Anthropic policy can block parts of the product

The Software Directory Policy bans **"Software that uses AI models to generate images, video, or audio content"**, except design-focused visual aids. The connector form also asks for an "AI media generation" acknowledgment ([Software Directory Policy](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13145358-anthropic-software-directory-policy); [Submit a connector](https://claude.com/docs/connectors/building/submission)). Silence cutting, transcript-based editing, text captions, reframing, color presets and timeline automation are editing, not generation, so they should be fine. **AI B-roll, AI voiceover and AI music** (like HookMaster's Fix Studio and Music) should stay *out* of the directory-listed plugin. You can offer them through your own marketplace or web app. Email Anthropic before you list anything that creates audio or video. The policy also says you must collect only the data you need, must not log conversation data, and must have a privacy policy if you connect to a remote service ([Software Directory Policy](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13145358-anthropic-software-directory-policy)). Remember that a directory plugin must be public, so do not put secrets in it. To submit at all, you need a paid Claude plan, a test account for reviewers, a docs URL, a privacy policy URL, a support contact and an icon ([Publish to the directory](https://claude.com/docs/directory/publish.md)).

### Payments from India need a Merchant of Record

**Stripe India has been invite-only for new accounts since May 2024**, and individual freelancers are rarely approved ([skydo](https://www.skydo.com/blog/stripe-india-charges-review-alternatives)). A **Merchant of Record (MoR)** sells on your behalf and handles VAT, GST and sales tax worldwide for about 4–5% plus about $0.40–0.50 per sale ([aliteq](https://aliteq.com/cheapest-merchant-of-record)). The options are:

- **Dodo Payments.** It is founded in India and charges 4% + $0.40, plus 1.5% on international cards. Indian INR and UPI payments cost 4% + 15¢ ([Dodo pricing](https://dodopayments.com/pricing)). A competitor claims Dodo can hold reserves or close accounts at a 0.5% monthly refund or chargeback rate ([creem.io](https://www.creem.io/blog/dodo-payments-review-hidden-fees-2026)). That matters if your refund policy is generous.
- **Paddle.** It costs 5% + $0.50 and is known for stability.
- **Polar.** It also starts at 5% + $0.50, with cheaper paid tiers, and fits developer-style open-core products ([agentaos](https://agentaos.ai/merchant-of-record-fees)).
- **Lemon Squeezy.** It is now owned by Stripe and is moving users toward Stripe Managed Payments, so building on it is a risk ([movemrr](https://movemrr.com/compare/merchant-of-record-migration/)).
- **Razorpay.** It is good for Indian UPI and INR buyers, with automated e-FIRC, but it is not an MoR, so global tax stays your job ([projectsupply](https://projectsupply.in/blog/stripe-vs-razorpay-india-saas-2026)).
- **Gumroad.** It charges 10% + $0.50, which is expensive after the first launch ([insightraider](https://insightraider.com/en/blog/gumroad-vs-whop)).

Note that Dodo's importer cannot move active subscriptions to another provider ([movemrr](https://movemrr.com/compare/merchant-of-record-migration/)). Moving later is painful, so choose carefully now. Talk to an Indian chartered accountant about your business registration, GST on export income, and e-FIRC and FEMA paperwork for foreign payments before money starts coming in. The research did not confirm which MoRs accept unregistered individuals in India.

### Legal pages and licenses

You need **Terms of Service, an EULA** (seats, agency and client use), **a privacy policy** that covers footage and transcripts (GDPR and India's DPDP Act), a **refund policy** that matches your MoR's rules, and a DPA for agency buyers. Check every font, music track, LUT and sound effect you bundle in a style. Many stock licenses forbid passing their files on inside template packs. Copy Yonko's good habit and tell users to use only footage and music they own, and to study other creators' reels only for technique ([Editing OS](https://yonkomedia.com/editing-os)). Yonko's no-refund rule ("difficulty using it" is not a reason for a refund) protects the seller, but it scares buyers. A short trial or a free tier is a cleaner way to cut refunds.

### Technical traps

**Claude usage limits are the biggest complaint.** One Max-plan user spent about 10% of a $200 plan's 5-hour limit on a single 37-second clip with four render passes ([clixie.ai](https://www.clixie.ai/blog/claude-code-video-editing)). Sending one frame every 2 seconds of a 10-minute video to Claude costs about 470k–800k tokens ([sprites.ai](https://www.sprites.ai/claude-video-editing)). Claude also cannot hear audio or cut to the exact frame by itself. It needs an outside transcript ([sprites.ai](https://www.sprites.ai/claude-video-editing)). So do the heavy work (transcription, silence detection, frame analysis) **inside your tools, not inside Claude's context**, and add a **storyboard approval step before rendering**, as HookMaster does. The other traps are these:

- **Premiere bridges are fragile.** One popular Premiere MCP says text overlays don't work, its APIs are buggy, CEP is deprecated, and installation needs unsigned extensions in debug mode ([Glama README](https://glama.ai/mcp/servers/@hetpatel-11/Adobe_Premiere_Pro_MCP/blob/b9fa9862ecb7be8d717cd63c4b32be594e13bd6c/README.md)). Export **FCPXML or EDL** files that Premiere and Resolve open, instead of controlling the app live.
- **Resolve automation from outside needs the paid Resolve Studio** ([mcpservers.org](https://mcpservers.org/en/servers/github-com-samuelgursky-davinci-resolve-mcp)).
- **CapCut has no official API**. Community tools only write draft files ([usecarly](https://www.usecarly.com/blog/capcut-mcp/)).
- **Remotion needs Node 18+ and FFmpeg**, and companies may need a paid license ([Glama](https://glama.ai/mcp/servers/endlessblink/clean-cut-mcp)).
- **HyperFrames has telemetry on by default** and was reported as "Windows coming soon" ([Nodi guide](https://nodi.global/en/academy/edit-video-with-claude-code-hyperframes)).

### Onboarding is where buyers will drop off

Your buyer needs a desktop computer, a paid Claude plan, Claude Code, and often Node, Python and ffmpeg. Yonko's product has no phone option and offers email setup help ([Editing OS](https://yonkomedia.com/editing-os)). Plan for a one-command installer, a `doctor` command that checks the setup, a compatibility table (Mac or Windows, editing app version, Claude version), and a short first-run video. On the pricing page, show the **total monthly cost** (Claude Pro + vibeediting) so nobody feels tricked. Third-party skill marketplaces now advertise security scans ([agensi.io](https://www.agensi.io/claude-marketplace)), so buyers worry about installing unknown code. Signed releases and a clear list of permissions help you sell.

## Grow with content, teams and a style marketplace

The MCP market is young. An estimated **fewer than 5% of 20,000+ MCP servers earn anything**, and successful ones report about $500–$3,000 a month per platform ([mcp-marketplace.io](https://mcp-marketplace.io/blog/state-of-mcp-monetization-2026)). These are vendor blogs, so read them as rough signals. The lesson is that software alone rarely makes a big business here. **Distribution and higher-price offers** do. The research found no tutorials or community at HookMaster and no promised updates at Yonko. Your plan to ship workflows and tutorials is a real advantage.

The extra revenue streams, from best fit to later stage, are these. (1) **Team and agency plans** with shared style libraries and client brand kits. Agencies already pay Descript $50–$65 a seat and Submagic up to $69 ([forasoft](https://www.forasoft.com/learn/ai-for-video-engineering/articles-ai/opus-clip-descript-submagic-captions-ai-video-editor-tools-2026)). HookMaster's brand-guideline checks show that agencies want this. (2) **A paid course or cohort** ("become an AI-assisted editor"). (3) **Done-for-you setup and custom workflows** for agencies, a high price for low volume. (4) **A style marketplace** where human editors sell their styles and you take a share, which needs volume first. (5) **An affiliate program** for editing YouTubers. (6) **White-label versions** for agencies or editing tools. A community can sit on Whop (no monthly fee at low volume) or Skool (cheaper above about $3,000–$5,000 a month) ([aifunnelinsider](https://aifunnelinsider.com/whop-vs-skool-2026/)).

For growth, the product is visual, so **before-and-after demo videos** on YouTube, Instagram and X are your best ads. Yonko proves that LinkedIn and Instagram can sell 500 copies in India in weeks ([LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/akshayshirsagar/)). Creators already use "comment EDIT on this Reel" funnels to give away a free skill ([milehightitleguy](https://www.milehightitleguy.com/post/claude-video-editing-skill)). List for free in Anthropic's directory. Anthropic's official marketplace is registered automatically in Claude Code and had 314 plugins on 28 September 2026 ([developersdigest](https://www.developersdigest.tech/blog/best-claude-code-skills-2026)). Also list in the official MCP Registry, Glama and mcp.so, and in curated lists like awesome-claude-video-skills ([GitHub](https://github.com/zhuyansen/awesome-claude-video-skills)). Collect emails from day one so you own the customer list. Offer India and lower-income-country pricing to widen the market. Support more than one agent (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor) so you depend less on Anthropic. Adobe's own connector is the more direct threat, so watch how deep its Premiere support grows.

## Starting plan: two tracks in parallel

Do these first, in about 1–2 days, before either track starts. **Buy Yonko's Editing OS for ₹1,499** and study it. Open HookMaster /motion and /editing in a normal browser and note the price and FAQ. Pick **one narrow first workflow**. ## Conclusion

The research changes the main question. It is not "MCP or plugin". It is **"where does the paid value live?"** Both Indian competitors proved that people will pay to have their AI agent edit video. Both chose one-time prices, and that leaves them with no money to fund the constant fixes their products need. Anything you ship as text will be copied, and the plumbing underneath is already free. So the lasting business is the parts that keep improving: a style system that learns each creator's look, server-side tools that save Claude usage, monthly new styles, and teaching that gets non-technical creators to their first finished reel. The hybrid model gives you the India impulse sale *and* MRR. It also turns the competitors' strengths (Yonko's low price, HookMaster's review loop) into features of one product.

Two risks matter more than the rest. Anthropic's ban on generative media in its directory means your most "exciting" AI features may have to live outside your main distribution channel. Adobe, Descript and other incumbents can add agent editing to subscriptions creators already pay for. Your defence against both is to be the best at *one* creator workflow, to support several agents and editing apps, and to own your audience through tutorials and email.

## Open questions to sharpen the plan

1. **Who is the first buyer?** Talking-head creators and founders (like Yonko), professional editors and agencies, or founders making product videos (like HookMaster)? Pick one for launch.
2. **Which output path comes first?** Code-based render (ffmpeg plus Remotion or HyperFrames), or export to Premiere, Resolve or CapCut for finishing?
3. **Will you host anything at launch** (transcription, style analysis, cloud render), or stay 100% local and add hosted Pro features later?
4. **Do you want generative features** (AI B-roll, voice, music)? If yes, they cannot go in the Anthropic directory listing, so where will they live?
5. **What is your legal setup in India?** Are you registered (proprietorship, LLP, Pvt Ltd), do you have GST, and which MoR will accept you?
6. **Is your main market India, global, or both?** This decides your currency, launch price and marketing channels.
7. **What audience do you have today?** LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube or an email list, and how big is it?
8. **Which AI clients must work on day one?** Claude Code only, or also Codex, Cursor and Claude Desktop?
9. **Will you open-source the local engine?** It helps trust and discovery, but competitors can copy it.
10. **How much time can you give to support** each week? Setup help on Mac and Windows will be the biggest support cost early on.
