## 1. Put your video in a folder

Make a new folder, for example `my-first-reel`, and put your raw video in it (e.g. `raw.mp4`). Open your AI agent in that folder.

## 2. Ask for the edit

Copy this prompt and change it to your liking:

```text
Edit raw.mp4 into a 9:16 Instagram reel:
- cut all silences, "umm"s and retakes
- add word-by-word captions, yellow highlight, bold font, centre of the screen
- add a title card at the start: "3 mistakes new creators make"
- add subtle zoom-ins on important lines
Show me the plan first, then render it to exports/reel.mp4
```

## 3. Approve the plan

The agent will first **show you a plan**: which parts it will cut, the caption style, and so on. Read it, and reply with changes ("keep the joke at 0:42") or say **"go ahead"**.

## 4. Review and fix

Watch `exports/reel.mp4`. Not happy? Just say it:

```text
Captions are too big, make them 20% smaller. And cut the part from 0:31 to 0:36.
```

## Tips

- **Save your style** so every video looks the same: *"Save this caption style as my default."*
- Shorter videos use less of your AI plan. Split long recordings into parts.
- Good audio = better captions. Record close to the mic.
