This policy explains how **{{BUSINESS_NAME}}** ("we") handles personal data when you visit {{DOMAIN}} or use vibeediting. We follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

## What we collect

| Data | When | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Name, email, phone | When you buy | To create your order, send your license key and support you |
| Payment details | When you pay | Handled by **Razorpay**. We never see or store your card or UPI details |
| License key, a random computer ID, app version | When vibeediting checks your license | To activate your license and enforce the computer limit |
| Basic website logs | When you visit | To keep the website secure and working |

## What we do **not** collect

- **Your videos.** vibeediting runs on your computer. Your footage is not uploaded to us.
- **Your AI chats.** Your conversations go directly between you and your AI provider (for example Anthropic, OpenAI or Cursor), under their privacy policies.

## Who we share data with

Only service providers that help us run vibeediting: Razorpay (payments), our database host, our email provider and our website host. We do not sell your data.

## How long we keep it

Order and license data is kept as long as your license is active and as required by Indian tax law. You can ask us to delete your data. We may keep invoices where the law requires it.

## Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw consent, by emailing {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}. We will reply within 30 days.
