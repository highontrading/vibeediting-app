vibe editing is digital software that is delivered instantly, so we cannot "take it back" like a physical product. We still want you to be happy with it.

## When you can get a refund

You can ask for a **full refund within 7 days** of purchase if:

- vibe editing does not work on your computer, **and**
- you contacted our support at {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} and we could not fix the problem.

## When we cannot refund

- You changed your mind after receiving the license key.
- You do not have a supported computer or a paid AI agent plan (these are listed before you pay).
- Your AI provider's usage limits, prices or features changed.
- The license key was shared or misused.

## How to ask for a refund

Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} from the email you bought with. Include your payment ID (it starts with `pay_`) and a short description of the problem.

## How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are made to your original payment method through Razorpay within **5–7 working days**. When a refund is made, the license key is deactivated.

## Cancellation

A one-time purchase cannot be cancelled after payment, because the license key is delivered instantly. If a payment failed but money was deducted, it is automatically reversed by your bank or Razorpay, usually within 5–7 working days.
