*Last updated: {{LAST_UPDATED}}*

vibeediting is a **digital product**. Nothing is shipped physically.

## How it is delivered

1. Right after a successful payment, your **license key** is shown on the screen.
2. The same key is **emailed** to the address you entered at checkout, usually within 2 minutes.
3. You install vibeediting by following the [tutorials](/docs). The software is downloaded through npm when you add it to your AI agent.

## Didn't get your key?

- Check your spam and promotions folders.
- Use [Resend my key](/help) to send it again.
- If you still don't have it after 1 hour, email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} with your payment ID.

## Delivery area

vibeediting can be bought and used from anywhere. Payments are accepted in Indian Rupees through Razorpay.
